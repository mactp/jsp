Friends of Jacob Smith Park

Caring for Jacob Smith Park — enhancing habitats, improving access and connecting the community.

Who are the Friends of Jacob Smith Park?

The Friends of Jacob Smith Park (FoJSP) are a Community Interest Group formed in 2008, shortly after JSP opened to the public.

We’re passionate about enhancing the park’s habitat to increase biodiversity and making improvements that help make every visit more enjoyable. See our recent projects.

FoJSP is run by a committee that links the local community with North Yorkshire Council (NYC). The committee are all volunteers, supported by a wider network of local residents who help with practical projects and events.

We work closely with NYC, which owns the park and is responsible for its upkeep — including grass cutting, tree management and wall maintenance.

What does FoJSP do?

Represent the community

Speak for local residents and park users on matters affecting the park.

Agree priorities

Work with NYC to deliver the yearly plan for the park’s upkeep and development.

Fundraising

Raise funds for projects and improvements outside the council’s core responsibilities.

Volunteering

Organise volunteer days for agreed enhancements and habitat work.

Public meetings

Hold open meetings twice a year to share progress and gather views.

Recent projects

Benches

Raised funds and installed seven new oak benches for visitors to relax and enjoy the surroundings.

Bat & bird boxes

Installed nesting boxes for bats, owls, robins and other species to boost biodiversity.

Himalayan balsam control

Reduced this invasive species over three years to restore native plant growth.

Information board

Installed near the park entrance to share its history and wildlife highlights.

Tree planting

Planted over 300 trees to create new woodland habitat and increase diversity.

Wildflowers & shrubs

Planted hundreds of native species to feed pollinators, add colour, and support wildlife year-round.

Community talks

Hosted local experts covering topics from bees and meadows to astronomy and wellbeing.

Ponds

Worked with Yorkshire Wildlife Trust to restore two wildlife ponds for newt and amphibian habitats.

Tree protection

Supported NYC in replacing fencing around veteran trees to protect their roots and preserve them.
