Help us keep Jacob Smith Park beautiful

North Yorkshire Council cover the costs of maintaining the park, such as grass cutting and repairing the walls, but enhancements are mainly organised and funded by the Friends of JSP (FoJSP).

We aim to achieve the best possible results on a very tight budget. All the committee and volunteers give their time for free, and we have achieved some amazing results. See Get involved for examples of our recent projects.

We need your help to fund future enhancement projects and meet our ongoing running costs, including public liability insurance (without which we could not run volunteering events) and website hosting.

If you enjoy visiting JSP, please help us maintain and improve it for your future visits. Please donate as much as you are able; we'd love you to set up a monthly standing order. Regular payments of £3, £5 or £10 a month would really help us. Or if you prefer, set up an annual standing order or make a one-off donation for whatever you like.

Ways to give

Jacob Smith Park is free to use but not to run; a monthly donation helps us to plan new projects and continue to improve the park for your future visits.
