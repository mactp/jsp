Your donations help us maintain and enhance Jacob Smith Park for everyone to enjoy.

Help us keep Jacob Smith Park beautiful

North Yorkshire Council cover the costs of maintaining the park, but enhancements are mainly organised and funded by the Friends of JSP (FoJSP).

We aim to achieve the best possible results on a very tight budget. All the committee and volunteers give their time for free, and we have achieved some amazing results — see Get Involved for examples of our recent projects.

We need your help to fund future enhancement projects and meet our ongoing running costs, including:

  • Public liability insurance, without which we could not run volunteering events
  • Website hosting

If you love the park and want to help us make it even better, please donate.
