Donate to help fund our work and keep Jacob Smith Park beautiful…

North Yorkshire Council cover the costs of maintaining the park but enhancements are mainly organised and funded by the Friends of JSP (FoJSP).

We aim to achieve the best possible results on a very tight budget. All the committee and volunteers give their time for free, and we have achieved some amazing results (see Get involved for examples of some of our recent projects).

We need your help to fund future enhancement projects. We also need your support to meet our ongoing running costs e.g.

  • Public liability insurance, without which we could not run volunteering events.
  • Website hosting.

If you love the park and want to help us make it even better, please donate. We’d love you to set up a monthly standing order; regular payments of £1, £3 or £5 a month would really help us. If you prefer, you could make a one-off donation for whatever you like.
