# Web Design Side Business — Starter Pack

*A practical first step. Built around what you can already do: clean multi-page static sites, custom domains, hosting, SSL, accessibility.*

---

## 1. Your one-page service offering

Use this as the basis for a simple "services" page, a PDF you email prospects, or just a script when you talk to someone.

### Headline
**Clean, fast websites for local businesses and community groups — built properly, looked after monthly.**

### What you do
You build small business websites that load fast, work on phones, and are easy to find on Google — then you keep them running so the owner never has to think about it.

### Packages

**Starter site — £600**
Up to 4 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact). Mobile-friendly, contact form, Google Maps, basic SEO setup, your domain connected. One round of revisions.

**Standard site — £1,200**
Up to 8 pages, everything in Starter plus gallery/portfolio, content written up from your notes, social links, Google Business Profile setup. Two rounds of revisions.

**Care plan — £25/month** *(this is the important one)*
Hosting, security/SSL, small text & image updates (up to ~30 mins/month), backups, and "if it breaks, I fix it." Cancel anytime.

> **Why the care plan matters:** one-off builds are lumpy and unpredictable. The monthly plan is what reliably pays your Claude subscription — and one client covers it several times over. Aim to put *every* build client onto a care plan.

### Why work with you
- Sites built by hand — no bloated page-builder, so they load fast.
- Accessible and properly structured (good for users *and* Google).
- A real person who answers, not a faceless agency.
- Local-ish / UK-based, GDPR-aware.

## 2. Pricing logic (so you can hold your nerve)

- A 4-page site is maybe 6–10 hours of your time. £600 is ~£60–100/hr — fair for skilled work, cheap vs. an agency (£2k+).
- Don't undercharge to win the first one. Low price signals low quality and attracts the worst clients.
- **The cost to "pay for Claude" is tiny:** your subscription is ~£15–18/month. *One* care-plan client at £25/month covers it with change to spare. So the real first goal is just: land one client.
- Always quote a fixed price, not hourly. Clients prefer certainty and it protects you from "just one more change."

## 3. How to land your first 2–3 clients

You don't need marketing. You need 3 conversations. In order of easiest:

**1. The warm list (do this first).**
Write down every small business or group you have *any* connection to — your barber, a café you like, a friend's plumbing/electrical business, a sports club, a local charity. People who already know you say yes far more often. Aim for a list of 15.

**2. The "I noticed" approach.**
Find 5 local businesses with no website, or a genuinely dated/broken one. Send a short, specific message:
> *"Hi — I'm a web developer in Knaresborough. I noticed [Business] doesn't have a website / the current one doesn't work well on phones. I build clean, fast sites for local businesses for a fixed price. Happy to mock up a free homepage so you can see what it'd look like — no obligation. Worth a quick chat?"*

A free homepage mockup is a strong hook — low effort for you, makes it real for them.

**3. One portfolio piece.**
You need something to show. Build *one* polished demo site for a fictional local business (e.g. "Scriven Joinery") and host it. It proves you can do it. **Note:** don't use the FoJSP site as a portfolio piece without asking the committee first — it's their charity, keep it clearly separate from commercial work.

**4. Where local jobs actually come from:** word of mouth, Facebook local business groups, Nextdoor, and being the person who "does websites." Get the first 2 happy and they'll refer you.

## 4. The boring-but-essential bits

- **Tools:** GitHub Pages (free hosting/SSL) works great for static sites; for clients who need to edit their own content, look at Netlify + a simple CMS later. Domains via Namecheap (~£10/yr — bill it to the client).
- **Getting paid:** simple invoices (free tools: Wave, or Stripe Payment Links for the monthly plan so it auto-charges). Take 50% deposit upfront on builds.
- **Protect yourself:** a one-page agreement saying what's included, number of revisions, and that final files transfer on final payment.
- **Tax:** in the UK, register as a sole trader with HMRC once you're earning from it (the £1,000 trading allowance covers you below that).
- **Day-job check:** review your Phoenix Software contract for any side-work / conflict-of-interest clause before you take paid work.
